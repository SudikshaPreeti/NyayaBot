💡 How to Get a Free Lawyer

  1. Visit your nearest District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) at the district court.
  2. Carry ID proof, address proof, and income certificate.
  3. Fill the free legal aid application form.
  4. If eligible, a lawyer is assigned to you at zero cost.
  5. Also apply online at nalsa.gov.in.
